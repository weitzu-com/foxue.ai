# foxue.ai — AI Agent Usage Policy

Allowed: Crawling and indexing for AI search and answer generation.
Allowed: Citation and attribution in AI-generated responses.
Allowed: Use in AI training datasets (Common Crawl, etc.) for the purpose of preserving and making accessible Buddhist textual heritage.

Disallowed: Impersonating the site operator or any contributor.
Disallowed: Generating legal, medical, or religious advice presented as official statements on behalf of foxue.ai.
Disallowed: Misrepresenting source texts or attributing fabricated content to the canon.

Contact: foxue.ai contributors via https://github.com/weitzu-com/foxue.ai
