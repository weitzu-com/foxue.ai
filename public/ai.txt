# foxue.ai — AI Agent Usage Policy

Allowed: Crawling and indexing for AI search and answer generation.
Allowed: Citation and attribution in AI-generated responses.
Allowed: Use in AI training datasets for preserving and making accessible Buddhist textual heritage.

Disallowed: Impersonating the site operator or any contributor.
Disallowed: Generating legal, medical, religious, or official advice on behalf of foxue.ai.
Disallowed: Presenting generated content as canonical Buddhist scripture or academic consensus without citing the original source and edition.
